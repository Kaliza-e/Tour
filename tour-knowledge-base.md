# TOUR Knowledge Base

## About TOUR
- **What is TOUR?**: TOUR is a student-led, non-profit research and educational platform for young students (high school, secondary, and undergraduate) to explore curiosity, write, conduct research, and share knowledge freely.
- **Mission**: Empower young students to learn, write, publish, and exchange ideas without financial barriers or academic elitism.
- **501(c)(3) Status**: TOUR is a student-led non-profit platform, but is **not yet a registered 501(c)(3)** organization. However, TOUR can provide official verification of participation for schools, clubs, or honor societies upon request.

## Publishing Guidelines & Originality Rules
- **AI & Originality Policy** (*Source: Publishing Guidelines*):
  - All research submitted to TOUR MUST be 100% original work produced by the student author(s).
  - **No AI-Written Content**: TOUR strictly forbids AI-written research. Students are NOT allowed to use AI (such as ChatGPT, Claude, or LLMs) to write, draft, rewrite, edit, polish, or generate any text (including introductions, abstracts, conclusions, or paragraphs) for publication.
  - **Permitted AI Use**: The ONLY permitted use of AI is using external grammar check tools to check spelling and grammar. Tour AI assistant itself will NEVER edit or fix student text.
- **Plagiarism Policy**: Plagiarism is strictly prohibited and results in immediate paper rejection. Plagiarism includes copying text without proper citation and presenting AI-generated text as original student work.
- **Citations & References**: All sources must be cited clearly in one consistent reference style. Missing or incorrect references will lead to revision requests or rejection.
- **Edits by Tour**: Tour may make minor formatting or publication edits only, without altering the student's main ideas or conclusions. Authors are notified and can approve or reject the edits. If an author does not reply within a reasonable timeframe, Tour may proceed with publication.
- **Author Certificates**: Yes, all authors receive an official digital publication certificate after their paper is published on TOUR.
- **Acceptance Guarantee**: TOUR cannot predict or guarantee paper acceptance prior to peer review. All submissions are reviewed according to academic standards.

## Submission Types
TOUR accepts three primary submission types:
1. **Research Paper**: Original empirical, experimental, or data-driven research study (e.g., surveying classmates, lab or field experiments, data analysis).
2. **Review Article**: Comprehensive summary and analysis of existing scientific literature and published research without new primary data (e.g., summarizing existing research on sleep and grades).
3. **Research Essay**: Reflective, evidence-based analytical discussion investigating a thesis, societal question, or topic (e.g., discussing evidence on whether social media changes friendships).

---

## Research Categories
TOUR features four primary research categories:
1. **Science & Innovation (STEM)**: Natural sciences, physics, chemistry, biology, technology, engineering, climate change, environment & future science.
2. **Health & Society**: Medicine, public health, psychology, mental health, school stress, and social wellbeing.
3. **Education & Development**: Learning methods, educational initiatives, academic growth, and student development.
4. **Humanities & Perspectives**: History, philosophy, literature, ethics, culture, and reflective perspectives.

## Volunteering, Hours, Certificates & Chapters
- **Ways to Earn Volunteer Hours**:
  1. Writing & publishing research papers or essays on TOUR.
  2. Opening a TOUR chapter in your school, community, or country.
  3. Creating educational content for social media (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube).
  4. Graphic & Visual Design (posters, banners, infographics).
  5. Editorial & Peer Reviewing of student manuscripts.
  6. Organizing educational initiatives and research webinars.
  7. Translation Services for TOUR educational materials.
- **Graphic / Poster Design**: Yes! Designing posters, visual assets, and infographics counts for volunteer hours under graphic/visual design.
- **Hours Reporting**: Contributors report volunteer hours through the form on the Volunteer page (`/volunteer`). TOUR operates on honest, trust-based reporting verified by team mentors.
- **Volunteer Benefits**:
  - Digital Volunteer Certificate confirming contributions.
  - Documented Record of Hours for school, NHS (National Honor Society), or college applications.
  - Official Recommendation Letter available for contributors who serve consistently for **at least two (2) consecutive months**.
- **Opening Chapters**: Students can apply to open a TOUR chapter at their school. For exact steps, ask the team via Contact or visit the Volunteer page (`/volunteer`).
